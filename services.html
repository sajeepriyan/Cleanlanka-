Electrician & Wiring

Electricians may help diagnose faults, install or replace fixtures and assess household electrical systems.

Common requests

Lighting and switch issues; socket or fan installation; fault inspection; maintenance planning; electrical safety assessments.

What to discuss before hiring

Explain the symptoms, the location, the age of the installation, and whether the supply is unstable. Ask about experience with the specific system, qualifications required by law, the estimate, and whether materials are included.

Quality & safety note

Electrical systems are hazardous. Do not attempt to repair live wiring yourself; use an appropriately qualified electrician and agree on safe isolation arrangements.

Plumbing & Water Works

Plumbing work ranges from water leaks and drainage problems to fixture replacement and routine maintenance.

Common requests

Taps and washbasins; pipes and joints; water-flow faults; bathroom fittings; blocked drains; simple inspection of tanks or supply lines.

What to discuss before hiring

Describe where the leak or blockage occurs, note any urgency or water damage, and ask whether the quote includes diagnostics, materials, travel and cleanup.

Quality & safety note

For significant water leaks, contamination or building damage, prioritise protecting people and property and seek a suitable professional promptly.

Service 03 · Sri Lanka

Home & Office Cleaning

Cleaning professionals can assist with planned or one-time tidying, surface care and organised premises maintenance.

Common requests

Kitchen and bathroom cleaning; room and floor care; move-in or move-out cleaning; workplace cleaning; scheduled domestic housekeeping.

What to discuss before hiring

Agree which rooms, areas and surfaces are included; whether cleaning supplies are provided; access arrangements; expected duration; and any delicate or restricted areas.

Quality & safety note

Tell the worker about allergies, pets and vulnerable surfaces. Keep dangerous products and personal valuables appropriately secured; cleaners should not be asked to handle unknown hazardous chemicals.

How to find a suitable service provider

1. Describe the task clearly

Start with the service needed, property type, district, timing and a simple description of the issue or request. If useful, clarify the number of rooms, equipment model, approximate size or preferred working hours without posting private identification details publicly.

2. Compare worker information

Check available profiles for relevant skills, practical experience, district, listed pricing and availability. Where qualifications or licences matter, request independent confirmation. A review or approval label is not a blanket guarantee.

3. Agree the scope and price

Before a visit, agree whether assessment, labour, supplies, transport, materials and cleanup are included. Confirm timing, access arrangements, payment expectations and the process for changes.

4. Prioritise safety and privacy

Use suitable professionals for specialist or regulated work. Avoid sharing identity document numbers, passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary private details in public job posts or ordinary chat.

Frequently asked questions

Can I find workers in any Sri Lankan district?

Clean Lanka is designed for all 25 districts, but currently available workers and open jobs depend on active registrations and eligibility. Use the district filter on the homepage.

Does Clean Lanka guarantee the listed price?

No. Listed service rates are for initial comparison. Confirm the total price, labour and material costs with the professional before agreeing to a task.

Is a listed worker automatically licensed or insured?

Not necessarily. Account approval and uploaded clearance documents are different from professional licensing, insurance or specialist certification. Check credentials independently where required.

How do workers find work opportunities?

Workers can browse public job opportunities, sign in and apply if their account meets current eligibility requirements. Scheduled official job posts may disappear after their expiration date.

How do customers and workers communicate?

Clean Lanka supports in-platform messaging for eligible conversations. Worker phone numbers are intended to be publicly visible only when the worker chooses that option.

How do I report an inaccurate listing or a safety issue?

Contact cleanlankaa@gmail.com, describing the concern and related listing. For emergencies, use the appropriate local emergency services rather than website chat.

Need help finding a professional?

Browse the homepage, choose a service and filter by district. Actual worker and job availability may vary.
