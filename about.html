Our purpose

Clean Lanka connects people looking for help at home or at work with independent service professionals. Our aim is to simplify discovery: instead of searching through scattered listings, customers can explore categories, review relevant profiles and make enquiries using one platform.

Workers can present their skills, experience, district, availability and indicative rates. Eligible workers can also explore public job opportunities. Clean Lanka itself is a marketplace, not a blanket promise that every listing will lead to a job, booking or particular outcome.

What you can find

Our service directory includes Electrician, Plumber, Cleaning, Hotel Staff, Security, Driver, Cooking, Carpenter, Gardening, AC Repair, Construction and Caregiver categories. Each category may include a range of tasks and specialisations.

Visit our Service Information Guides for practical examples of typical work, questions to ask before hiring and relevant safety considerations. Actual professionals and open jobs depend on district and availability.

For customers

  • Browse service categories and search by service keyword and district.
  • Review the information available in eligible worker profiles.
  • Post genuine job opportunities with district, service description, preferred date and budget where appropriate.
  • Communicate through Clean Lanka's messaging features when enabled for your account.
  • Confirm work scope, qualifications, price, dates and safety arrangements with the professional.

Sri Lankan coverage

Clean Lanka is intended for customers and workers throughout Sri Lanka's 25 districts, including Batticaloa, Colombo, Gampaha, Galle, Jaffna, Kandy, Matara, Kurunegala, Trincomalee and the other districts. District selection helps narrow searches; coverage does not guarantee an active worker in every trade or area.

The website offers English content and includes language-selection functionality for Sinhala and Tamil in supported parts of the experience.

Profiles, clearance and trust

Worker verification and approval are platform review steps, not a guarantee that a person is insured, licensed, background-clear for every purpose or suitable for a particular task. Some worker profiles may carry pending-clearance status during a limited period. Customers should independently confirm appropriate skills, licences and references where the task calls for them.

Private identity and clearance documents should not appear in public worker profiles. Please see the Privacy Policy for our intended handling of those records. For potentially hazardous or regulated work, choose an appropriately qualified professional.

How the marketplace works

1. Find the right category

Choose a service such as Cleaning or Plumbing, or search by keyword and district.

2. Compare available listings

Review approved profiles and open jobs. The information shown depends on the user's preferences, approval status and current records.

3. Agree the details

Contact the other party through available Clean Lanka features and agree the specific work, dates, pricing and conditions before starting.
