1. The marketplace

Clean Lanka is an online platform for discovering home and domestic service professionals, viewing job opportunities and exchanging service enquiries in Sri Lanka. Registered customers can post eligible jobs; registered service professionals may apply and communicate where their accounts are eligible.

Clean Lanka facilitates introductions and platform communication. Unless expressly stated for a particular engagement, a worker and customer agree the specific work and its terms between themselves. A profile or job listing is not a guarantee that any particular booking or job will occur.

2. Accounts and eligibility

Provide accurate registration information, use a valid email address, keep your password confidential and contact us if you suspect account misuse. Accounts must be used only by people legally entitled to enter the relevant arrangements. Workers must meet applicable Sri Lankan work-eligibility and minimum-age laws, and must not claim qualifications or licences they do not hold.

Customers and workers are responsible for keeping their own account information up to date. Creating misleading, impersonated or duplicate accounts to evade restrictions is not permitted.

3. Worker profiles and review status

Workers may be asked to provide service details, contact information, identity photographs and supporting clearance documents for an administrative review. Approval may depend on submitted information and platform checks; some profiles may have pending documentation or a time-limited clearance window. Any “approved” or “verified” label should not be interpreted as an unconditional guarantee of reliability, professional licensing, insurance or personal safety.

Workers must not upload another person's identification, forge records, misuse someone else's photo, or falsely advertise specialist skills. A worker can choose whether to show a phone number publicly where the platform offers that setting. Identity documents should remain in the designated private upload process, not in public descriptions.

4. Jobs, applications and bookings

Job advertisements should provide a truthful service description, accurate district, reasonably complete requirements and budget information where relevant. A preferred work date is different from an advertisement's publication period. Official Clean Lanka opportunities may be published for a scheduled window and will cease to appear publicly after expiry, without necessarily deleting related records.

Workers should apply only to suitable jobs and must not submit unsolicited spam or misleading experience claims. Customers should review an applicant's qualifications and confirm the scope, payment, dates, materials and service expectations before proceeding. Job statuses, assignment and completion depend on the platform process and participants' actions.

5. Prices, work quality and payments

Profile rates and job budgets are indicative unless the parties explicitly agree otherwise. Before work begins, confirm any visit fee, labour price, materials, transport costs, taxes, cancellation arrangements and payment method. Do not assume a payment is protected by an escrow, insurance or refund service unless Clean Lanka clearly advertises that specific protection.

Customers and workers remain responsible for lawful performance, agreed payments and keeping appropriate receipts or records. Do not send money or personal information in response to suspicious demands.

6. Safety and professional conduct

Communicate respectfully and use the marketplace for lawful services only. Threats, harassment, discriminatory abuse, fraud, unsafe conduct and requests for illegal activity are not allowed. For electrical, gas, structural, medical or other regulated tasks, use suitably qualified professionals and check any legally required authorisations.

Customers should take normal precautions when inviting an unfamiliar person into their home. Workers should assess the safety of a worksite and decline tasks that are unlawful or beyond their competence. Report serious concerns to appropriate local authorities and contact Clean Lanka if a platform account or listing is involved.

7. Messages and submitted content

Users are responsible for job postings, profile descriptions, messages, photographs and other materials they submit. Do not post misleading claims, copyrighted materials without permission, another person's confidential information or offensive or unlawful content.

Clean Lanka may moderate content, investigate reports, suspend listings or restrict access where needed for platform integrity and safety, subject to applicable law. Users should avoid sharing passwords, full identity document numbers or banking credentials through ordinary chat.

8. Changes, cancellations and disputes

Booking and application status changes should follow the platform rules and any separate lawful agreement between the customer and worker. The ability to cancel or reassign may depend on job status and account permissions. An advertisement expiring from public view does not automatically resolve completed work, payments or existing applications.

For a disagreement, preserve relevant messages and agreed terms, contact the other participant respectfully and seek help through the platform contact address when appropriate. Clean Lanka may review reports, but does not promise to decide every private contract dispute.

9. Privacy and account security

Use of account, message and verification information is described in our Privacy Policy. Worker identity documents and other private verification material must not be copied into public listings. Users must not attempt to access another account, bypass approval steps or interfere with platform systems.

10. Availability and updates

We work to keep the website available, but features, worker availability and job opportunities may change or occasionally be interrupted. We may update site content, service categories and these terms where appropriate. We do not promise a fixed number of jobs or workers in every district.

Mandatory legal rights and protections are not waived by these terms. Applicable Sri Lankan law governs where permitted by law, without removing rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
