Read Worker Terms & Conditions

Clean Lanka — Worker Terms & Conditions (Version 1.0, 8 November 2026). These rules apply to worker accounts and services arranged through Clean Lanka in Sri Lanka.

1. Eligibility and honesty. Workers must be at least 18 years old, legally permitted to provide the services offered, and must supply accurate names, contact details, skills, qualifications, experience and availability. Impersonation, fake reviews, forged identification, false certificates and misleading service claims are prohibited.

2. Verification and approval. Clean Lanka may request identity and supporting documents and may approve, reject, suspend or re-review profiles for reasonable safety and integrity concerns. Approval does not guarantee a worker's skill, conduct, employment, income, or the safety of any booking. Workers must keep details up to date.

3. Safe and lawful work. Workers must follow applicable Sri Lankan laws, safety requirements and any necessary licensing or qualification rules. Theft, threats, harassment, discrimination, abuse, fraud, bribery, unsafe work and illegal activities are prohibited. Workers must respect homes, property and customer boundaries and report serious safety incidents promptly.

4. Bookings and prices. Workers must describe the work, price, materials, schedule and any changes truthfully and obtain customer agreement before extra charges. No deceptive charges, fake bookings, payment scams or misuse of customer information. Any platform fees, cancellation terms or payment arrangements must be disclosed before they apply; none are created merely by these Terms.

5. Communications and confidentiality. Use the permitted Clean Lanka communication and booking channels. Do not publish customer addresses, phone numbers, private messages, photos or documents without lawful authority and permission. Do not solicit or pressure customers or misuse access to their premises.

6. Account security and evidence. Keep login credentials confidential; report suspected account misuse. Only upload documents you are authorised to provide. Public portfolio photos and reviews must be genuine, appropriately permitted and subject to moderation. Clean Lanka may investigate reports, preserve relevant records where lawful and cooperate with competent authorities.

7. Complaints and enforcement. Customers and workers may report problems to cleanlankaa@gmail.com. Clean Lanka may request evidence and restrict accounts while investigating. Users may respond to allegations. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal, without limiting rights or remedies under Sri Lankan law.

8. Platform role and liability. Clean Lanka connects customers and independent service providers unless a specific booking expressly states otherwise. The platform does not promise uninterrupted service or risk-free outcomes. Nothing in these Terms excludes liability or consumer, worker or privacy rights that cannot legally be excluded.

9. Changes and governing law. Material changes will be notified appropriately and may require renewed acceptance. Sri Lankan law applies, subject to mandatory legal protections and the jurisdiction of competent courts.

Read Worker Privacy Policy

Clean Lanka — Worker Privacy Policy (Version 1.0, 8 November 2026). Contact: cleanlankaa@gmail.com.

1. Information collected. Registration and profile details may include name, email, telephone, district, service area, gender if provided, skills, experience, prices, availability, account identifiers, verification documents, selfie, booking messages, reviews and technical/security records.

2. Why information is used. To create accounts, authenticate users, verify workers, manage bookings and communications, prevent fraud, handle complaints, secure the platform and meet applicable legal obligations. Processing must have an appropriate lawful basis under applicable Sri Lankan data protection law.

3. Public visibility. Approved worker profiles may show professional name, service area, skills, experience, rates, availability and approved portfolio/reviews. Phone numbers are displayed publicly only if the worker enables the public-phone option. Identification documents, police clearances, Grama Niladhari certificates and private verification selfies must never be placed on public profiles.

4. Who can access data. Authorised administrators and necessary technology providers may access data only for legitimate purposes under suitable safeguards. Booking participants may see information necessary for the service. Information may be disclosed where legally required. Clean Lanka does not promise that data will never be transferred or accessed outside Sri Lanka; any applicable transfer requirements must be respected.

5. Storage and security. Reasonable access controls and safeguards should protect accounts and private documents. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for their purposes and applicable legal duties, then deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

6. Choices and rights. Workers can request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights, and withdraw optional consent where legally available, by emailing cleanlankaa@gmail.com. Requests may be subject to lawful exceptions and identity checks. Where applicable, complaints may be made to Sri Lanka's Data Protection Authority.

7. Updates. Material policy changes will be communicated appropriately. Optional marketing communications, if introduced, should have a separate choice rather than being bundled into this registration agreement.
