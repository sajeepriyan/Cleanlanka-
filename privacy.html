Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information we handle when you use Clean Lanka, how it supports the marketplace, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Applies to cleanlanka.lk and related marketplace services

1. Who we are

Clean Lanka is an online marketplace connecting households and other customers with people offering home and domestic services across Sri Lanka. Our public website is cleanlanka.lk. Contact us at cleanlankaa@gmail.com or +94 70 280 5105. Our website lists Kallady, Batticaloa, Sri Lanka as its contact location.

We aim to handle information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Sri Lankan data protection and consumer laws. This policy describes the platform's intended information practices; it does not replace any additional notices presented when you register as a customer, worker, or administrator.

2. Information we collect

Customers

When you register or use the platform, we may collect your name, email address, district, phone number, residential or service address, account identifier, job requests, dates, budget, messages and related booking details.

Service professionals

Worker registrations may include name, email, telephone number, district, service area or address, selected skills, experience, offered pricing, availability, photographs and account details. For review purposes we may also collect private identity and supporting documents such as NIC images, police-clearance records, Grama Niladhari certificates and verification selfies.

Communications and administrative records

We may retain marketplace chat messages, job applications, request and assignment status, service feedback, safety reports, administrator permissions, account confirmation status and audit records where needed to operate and protect the service.

Technical information

Our hosting, authentication and security systems may receive IP address, browser/device details, timestamps, session identifiers and error or security logs as part of ordinary website operations.

3. How we use information

  • Create accounts, confirm email addresses, enable sign-in and password recovery.
  • Display appropriate worker profiles and public job opportunities, and connect marketplace participants.
  • Process job applications, service requests, messaging and availability settings.
  • Review worker documents and manage eligibility or approval decisions.
  • Detect misuse, investigate reports, apply platform rules and maintain an audit trail.
  • Maintain the website, troubleshoot errors, respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Processing may rely on consent, providing requested services, legitimate operational needs or legal requirements, as applicable. We do not represent that every listed worker has a particular professional licence or independent certification.

4. Public profiles and private documents

Approved worker listings may show a professional's name, district, skills, experience, rates and availability. A worker phone number is intended to appear publicly only where the worker enables the public-phone setting. Public job advertisements should not display a customer's private residential address or contact details.

Identity documents, police-clearance records, Grama Niladhari certificates and private verification photographs are collected for authorised review, not for public publication. Only authorised staff and necessary service providers should access them for legitimate platform purposes. If you discover a private document exposed publicly, please report it immediately.

5. Sharing information and service providers

Information needed for an enquiry, a job or a chat may be accessible to the participating customer, worker and appropriately authorised administrators. Clean Lanka uses technology providers, including Supabase for database, account and storage services and Vercel for website delivery; such providers may process information to operate the platform.

Data may be processed or stored outside Sri Lanka depending on service-provider infrastructure. We may also disclose information when necessary to meet a legal obligation, protect users or investigate platform misuse. We do not publish private verification documents as advertising content.

6. Cookies, sessions and advertising

Account authentication and website functions may use session storage, cookies or similar browser technologies. Some third-party assets and service providers may process basic technical information needed to display and operate the website.

Google AdSense is not currently installed on Clean Lanka. If advertisements are introduced, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads. We will update our notices and implement any legally required consent controls before enabling advertising. Learn more at Google's advertising technology explanation and My Ad Center.

Users should not assume that all advertising cookies are already active: this section explains potential future use.

7. Security and retention

We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards designed to reduce the risk of unauthorised access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and contact us about suspected misuse.

We aim to keep account, message, application, verification and administrative records only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, dispute handling, safety, legal requirements and appropriate retention periods. Deleting an account may not immediately remove records that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and identity verification. You can also ask questions about our processing or complain about a privacy concern by emailing cleanlankaa@gmail.com. Include your account email and a brief explanation; do not send your password or full identity-document numbers in an ordinary email.

Workers can manage the public-phone preference through the profile controls where available. Account data and messages can also be subject to platform security and legal requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as services and legal requirements develop. The updated date at the top indicates the version displayed. Where appropriate, we will provide further notice or request renewed agreement for material changes.
